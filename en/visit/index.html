Questions before you visit

Where is Serina?

Serina is on Thaniya Road in Bangkok’s Silom district. See the address and the entrance on the Visit page, then open Google Maps for directions.

How do I request a reservation?

Write a direct enquiry on the Reservations page and send it by email, call the restaurant, or follow the link to Hungry Hub for a package. A direct enquiry needs confirmation from the restaurant.

Do prices include service charge and VAT?

Menu prices on this website exclude the 10% service charge and 7% VAT, which are added to the bill. The same note appears beside the prices on the menu.

Is parking available?

Please confirm current parking arrangements with the restaurant before driving.

Can I visit with children or someone who needs step-free access?

Tell the restaurant your requirements before your visit so you can confirm the seating and access arrangements.

Can you accommodate dietary requirements?

Contact the restaurant before your visit to discuss the ingredients and preparation relevant to your needs.
