Reservations

There are two ways to arrange a meal at Serina. Enquire with the restaurant directly about a table and the seating you would like, or book one of the packages offered through Hungry Hub. A direct enquiry is a request: your table is confirmed only when the restaurant has replied to you.

How we use your reservation information

Questions before you visit

How do I request a reservation?

Write a direct enquiry on the Reservations page and send it by email, call the restaurant, or follow the link to Hungry Hub for a package. A direct enquiry needs confirmation from the restaurant.

Can I request counter seating?

Tell the restaurant your seating preference when enquiring. If that seating is essential, say so before confirming the arrangement.

Are website prices the same as Hungry Hub packages?

The restaurant menu and external packages may have different inclusions and conditions. Check each offer’s final price and details on the channel where you book.

How do I change a booking?

Use the channel where the booking was made and provide its reference, if available. Check that the change has been confirmed.
