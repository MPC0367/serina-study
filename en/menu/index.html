Menu

Courses, steaks and small plates

Six teppanyaki courses first, then steaks, seafood and single plates from the teppan. A second menu has shabu-shabu, sukiyaki and small dishes.

Menu prices in baht. A 10% service charge and 7% VAT are added to the bill. For allergies or dietary needs, please ask the restaurant about ingredients and preparation before ordering.

English, Thai or Japanese — for example beef, กุ้ง or 牛.
