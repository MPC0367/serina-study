Privacy

This page describes what this website does with information — which is very little.

The enquiry form

The reservation enquiry form writes a message in your browser. The website does not send it or store it on a server. It reaches the restaurant only if you send it from your own email app, or read it out by phone. When browser storage is available, a draft stays in this tab across reloads and language changes until you clear it or close the tab. If saving is blocked, fields remain only on the current page; copy your details before leaving or reloading. Meal-plan links share course choices only; they do not include your name or contact details.

Enquiries you send

An enquiry you send goes to Serina Japanese Teppanyaki by the route you choose, and is handled by the restaurant in order to answer you and arrange your visit. To ask about, correct or remove what you have sent, contact the restaurant by the same route.

Other services

Links to Google Maps, Hungry Hub, Chope, Tripadvisor, Instagram and Facebook open those services in a new tab. What they collect is governed by their own terms. One thing can be loaded into this website from another service: the Google map on the home page and the Visit page. It loads automatically when you scroll near the map. This contacts Google, which receives your IP address and may use its own cookies. The address and external directions link remain available if the embed is blocked or slow.

Cookies and measurement

This website sets no advertising or analytics cookies and does not measure visits. The automatically loaded Google map may use Google’s own cookies. If visit measurement is added later, this page will say so before it is switched on.
