About

About Serina

Serina Japanese Teppanyaki is in Bangkok’s Thaniya neighbourhood. Explore the menu, look around the restaurant, and find the practical details for your visit.

In Thaniya

The restaurant is on Thaniya Road, in the Silom district — a street long known for its Japanese restaurants. Above a tall wooden door, three square signs carry its name in brush-written Japanese, 瀬里奈, with three words beneath them: Teppanyaki, Japanese, Dining.

What we serve

The menu starts with teppanyaki: six set courses built around Kagoshima and Miyazaki beef, US beef, Hokkaido pork and seafood, then steaks, prawns and vegetables cooked on the teppan one plate at a time. A second menu adds shabu-shabu and sukiyaki, and the small dishes — cold plates, grilled fish, tempura, pickles, rice and soup — that make up a Japanese table.
