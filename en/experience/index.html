Experience

The experience

Teppanyaki is cooked on the teppan — a flat iron griddle. At Serina the meal is built around it, in a quiet room in Thaniya.

The room

A counter runs alongside the teppan, with dark wood and warm light around it. Tables are set nearby, and the entrance is a single wooden door beneath the restaurant’s three signs on Thaniya Road.

The meal

Each of the six set courses centres on one main ingredient — Kagoshima or Miyazaki beef, US beef, Hokkaido pork, or seafood — and is served with a starter, salad, soup, grilled vegetables, garlic rice and dessert. Beyond the courses, the menu offers steaks, prawns and single plates from the teppan, and a second menu covers shabu-shabu, sukiyaki and small dishes.
